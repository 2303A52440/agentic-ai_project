First-Aid & Emergency Action Protocols

Standard clinical immediate actions for acute injuries and common medical events.

R.I.C.E.

Sprains & Soft Tissue Strains

  • Rest: Stop bearing weight on the joint.
  • Ice: Cold pack wrapped in cloth for 15-20 min every 2-3 hours.
  • Compress: Elastic bandage wrap (firm, not cutting circulation).
  • Elevate: Raise limb above heart level to decrease edema.

Thermal Burns (1st & 2nd Degree)

  • Cool Water: Immediately run cool tap water for 10-15 minutes (Never ice!).
  • Protect: Cover loosely with clean, non-stick sterile gauze.
  • Never Pop: Do not puncture blisters or apply butter/oils.
  • Seek Care: If burn involves face, hands, or exceeds 3 inches.
F.A.S.T.

Acute Stroke Recognition

  • Face: Ask the person to smile. Does one side droop?
  • Arms: Raise both arms. Does one drift downward?
  • Speech: Ask them to repeat a simple phrase. Is it slurred?
  • Time: Call emergency response (911/112) immediately!
